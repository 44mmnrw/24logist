# Formats and layout routing

Choose a destination-specific layout. Dimensions are defaults when the user does not supply an exact size. The safe zone applies to all text, logos, badges, and essential illustrations; only nonessential decoration may bleed beyond it.

## Blog

- Hero/cover: `1600x900`, 16:9.
- Wide inline infographic: `1600x900` or `1600x1000` when a diagram needs more vertical room.
- Keep critical content at least 72 px from every edge.
- Do not add side fields or letterboxing.

## Telegram

- Default post infographic: `1280x1280`, 1:1.
- Article-preview alternative: `1280x720`, 16:9, only for a short headline and one supporting visual.
- Keep essential content at least 88 px from every edge.
- Test the final raster at 320 px wide. The headline and dominant number or status must remain readable without opening the image.

## MAX

- Default post infographic: `1280x1280`, 1:1.
- Keep essential content at least 88 px from every edge.
- Do not add a `MAX` badge unless explicitly requested.
- Test at 320 px wide and verify that no right-aligned label or capsule approaches the crop boundary.

Telegram and MAX may share a master when their canvas, safe zones, and content are identical, but export separate platform filenames rather than stretching a raster.

## Vertical promotional card

- Default: `1080x1350`, 4:5.
- Use for one offer, one key number, one short headline, no more than two benefits, and one CTA.
- Build around the number or offer; do not place equal emphasis on every sentence.

## Announcement or release card

Use a square editorial hero:

1. Logo in the upper safe area.
2. Optional short date or category marker.
3. One headline of no more than three lines.
4. One supporting sentence.
5. One logistics/document visual occupying roughly one third of the canvas.

Do not add a status badge unless it conveys information not already stated by the headline.

## Three-column comparison

Use this structure for three parallel scenarios, actors, or options:

1. A shared high-contrast header band with the title and a small process motif.
2. Three equal cards with distinct semantic accents such as orange, teal, and blue.
3. In each card: number, short title, actor flow, document labels, and no more than two supporting notes.
4. A shared dark value band and a CTA footer only when they genuinely apply to every column.

At mobile/social sizes, shorten labels before shrinking type. If columns become unreadable at a 320 px preview, create a vertical stacked version.

## Text limits

- Main title: preferably 4–12 words and no more than three lines.
- Card title: 2–6 words.
- Body label: one short sentence or two compact lines.
- CTA: 2–5 words.
- Capsule or badge: preferably one or two short words; size it from measured content.

If the supplied article cannot fit these limits, summarize it into a visual outline and keep the full explanation in the post or article.
